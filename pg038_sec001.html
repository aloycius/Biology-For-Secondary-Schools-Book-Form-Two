Activity 1.9: Investigate the solubility of protein in water and effects of heat on proteins

Materials

Egg white, water, test tubes, measuring cylinder (10 mL), test tube holder, and heat source

Procedure

1.

Pour 3 mL of water into a test tube

followed by 2 mL of egg white.

2.

Shake the mixture.

3.

Record your observations.

4.

Heat the mixture for 2-3 minutes.

Safety precautions

Ensure proper ventilation for

heating safely. Avoid flammable

substances near the heating

source. Place your burner on

the bench, never hold it in your

hands. Use test tube holder to

hold the test tube

5.

Observe and record your findings.
