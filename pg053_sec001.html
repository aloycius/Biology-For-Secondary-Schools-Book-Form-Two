Search the library and internet sources for

The overall process of digestion involves

the break down of large and complex food

substances into small, simple, digestible,

soluble, absorbable, and usable food

molecules. Foods with mineral sources

are broken and finally release mineral

elements such as potassium, sodium, iron,

magnesium, iodine, calcium, chloride,

fluoride, and nitrate, as shown in Table

2.2. Therefore, vitamins and minerals are

released to the body as the food is being digested. For example, the digestion of meat will release amino acids together with mineral salts.
