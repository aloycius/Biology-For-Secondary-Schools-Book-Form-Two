Introduction

Respiration is one of the characteristics of living things. It is important for the

survival of living things as it leads to the production of energy used for various

body activities. Respiration in aerobic organisms is accomplished by gas

exchange. Gas exchange involves the exchange of oxygen and carbon dioxide

gases across the respiratory surface. In this chapter, you will learn about the

mechanisms of gas exchange and respiration in mammals, including aerobic and

anaerobic respiration. The competences developed will enable you to maintain a

healthy life style that helps to prevent infections and diseases of the respiratory

system.

Gas exchange is a physical process

by which gases move passively by

diffusion across a surface, such as the

exchange of oxygen gas and carbon

dioxide gas across the respiratory

surface. This takes place in different

ways in various organisms. For

example, unicellular organisms carry

out gas exchange by diffusion across

the cell membrane. Large organisms

cannot carry out diffusion efficiently;

instead, they have well developed and
