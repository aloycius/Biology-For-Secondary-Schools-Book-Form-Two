Activity 1.7 (a): Test for the presence

of lipids in groundnuts by the grease

spot test

Materials

Groundnuts seeds and a piece of white paper

Procedure

1.

Peel the groundnut seeds.

2.

Rub the peeled seeds on a piece

of paper.

3.

Expose the paper to light.

4.

Compare the part of the paper

rubbed with seeds with the part

that was not rubbed.

5.

Record what you see.

6.

Tabulate your results showing

food sample tested, procedures,

observation and inference.

Activity 1.7 (b): Test for the presence of lipids in cooking oil by using Sudan

III reagent

Materials

Sudan III reagent, 5 mL of cooking oil, test tube, measuring cylinder (10 mL), and water

Procedure

1.

Pour 3 mL of water in the test tube

followed by 2 mLs of cooking oil.

2.

Add 2 drops of Sudan III reagent in the mixture.

3.

Shake the mixture well.

4.

Leave the mixture for 3-5 minutes

to settle.
