Nutritional deficiencies and disorders

Nutritional deficiencies

These deficiencies arise when the body

does not have a sufficient supply of

particular food nutrients. Examples

of common nutritional deficiencies

in humans include marasmus (acute

malnutrition), kwashiorkor (acute

malnutrition with oedema), and rickets.

Marasmus

Marasmus is a form of malnutrition in

children caused by a lack of adequate

amounts of food nutrients, leading to

starvation.

Symptoms of marasmus: A child

suffering from marasmus shows weight

loss, slowed growth, decreased activity

and a lack of energy. He or she also has

wrinkled skin and extreme hunger. The

child also cries often.
