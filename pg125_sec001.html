Diseases and disorders of the human

circulatory system

Diseases and disorders of the human

circulatory system affect blood, blood

vessels, and the heart. Diseases and

disorders may be caused by genetic

factors, unhealthy eating habits, and other

lifestyles. Eating food with high levels

of cholesterol and fat causes narrowing

of blood vessels due to their deposition

in blood vessels. Stress and lifestyles

such as smoking, lack of exercise, and

alcohol consumption cause a great risk

of developing heart diseases, including

coronary heart disease and high blood

pressure.

Arteriosclerosis

Arteriosclerosis is the hardening of

arteries. It happens when there are fat

depositions on the wall of the artery

or when fibrous tissues form in the

artery wall causing the narrowing of

the lumen of the artery (Figure 5.18).

It can also occur when the artery wall

degenerates. This hinders the normal

working of the artery, affecting the

efficiency of blood flow.
