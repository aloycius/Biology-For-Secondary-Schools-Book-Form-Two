Monocotyledonous roots

Monocot plants have fibrous roots.

This type of roots form a wide network

of thin roots that originate from the

stem, and they do not grow deep into

the soil but remain close to the surface.

The vascular structures are arranged

in a circular pattern around the central

pith, as shown in Figure 4.6. The

pith consists of ground tissue called

parenchyma.
