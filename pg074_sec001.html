The importance of osmosis

Osmosis is an important process in both plants and animals. In plants, osmosis aids the absorption of water by the roots. Osmosis also aids the opening and closing of the stomata. When guard cells absorb water the stomata open. When they lose water, the stomata close. Osmosis also plays a key role during seed germination. It enables the absorption of water that softens the seed coat, hence, increasing seed permeability for dissolved minerals and oxygen. This promotes the growth of seed embryo during germination.
