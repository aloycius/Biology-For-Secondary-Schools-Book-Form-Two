Surface area to volume ratio: The

larger the surface area to volume ratio,

the larger the number of particles that

will be able to move in a given time,

hence, the higher the diffusion rate.

Distance over which diffusion takes

place: When the distance over which

the material is transported is long,

the rate of diffusion decreases. If the

distance is short, diffusion occurs faster

because the materials do not have to

travel far. For example, in a thin layer

of cells, the rate of diffusion increases.

a notebook

Procedure
