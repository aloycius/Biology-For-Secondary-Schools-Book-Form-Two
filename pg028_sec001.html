Sometimes children may suffer from obesity (childhood obesity) when their body weight is very high in relation to their age and height. The child becomes highly obese due to an excessive accumulation of fat in the body.

Causes of obesity

The causes of obesity include overeating, inactivity, eating high amounts of energy foods especially fats and sugars, frequent eating, and genetic makeup.

Some people have genes that affect how foods are processed in the body.
