(i)

They are thin in order to reduce

the diffusion distance.

(ii) They are moist in order to dissolve

gases that diffuse in a solution

form.

(iii) They are highly branched, folded,

or flattened in order to increase the

surface area for gas exchange.

(iv) They are surrounded by blood

capillaries so that gases can be

taken to and from the cells easily.

(v)

They are well ventilated so that

gases can pass through them

easily.

in different organisms.

Record their similarities and
